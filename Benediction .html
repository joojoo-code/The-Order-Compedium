The Order

A visual compendium of the Order, its paths, traditions, and service.

❦
I
❦

The Order

The Order is a spiritual institution devoted to the preservation, practice, and service of sacred tradition.

The Council

The Order is guided by a council whose members represent its principal responsibilities.

Four principal seats
  • Head of the Order
  • Leader of the Spiritual Path
  • Leader of the Scholarly Path
  • Leader of the Keepers

The leaders of the three paths therefore sit within the wider leadership of the Order rather than standing apart from it.

The Hands serve across the three paths and do not constitute a fourth path of the Order.

II
❦

The Spiritual Path

The Spiritual Path concerns the practice and study of the sacred, including prayer, intercession, ritual, and spiritual phenomena.

Its Members
  • Practitioners of spiritual disciplines
  • Those trained in ritual and spiritual practice
  • Those who study spiritual phenomena
  • Those who undertake medium training
Leadership

The leader of the Spiritual Path sits on the Council of the Order.

The Oracle

The Oracle is chosen from the Spiritual Path and holds a distinct sacred role within it.

III
❦

The Scholarly Path

The Scholarly Path is concerned with knowledge: its preservation, interpretation, teaching, and continued study.

Its Members
  • Scholars and teachers
  • Researchers of history and doctrine
  • Interpreters of sacred texts
  • Preservers of learned tradition
Leadership

The leader of the Scholarly Path sits on the Council of the Order.

IV
❦

The Keepers

The Keepers safeguard the physical places, records, and objects entrusted to the Order.

Their Duties
  • Care for sacred places and grounds
  • Preserve records and archives
  • Safeguard sacred objects and relics
  • Guide visitors and pilgrims
Leadership

The leader of the Keepers sits on the Council of the Order.

V
❦

The Hands

The Hands are members drawn from all three paths and entrusted with service beyond the boundaries of a single path.

Their Work
  • Travel where the Order's service is needed
  • Assist communities and visitors
  • Carry out practical missions
  • Teach, guide, and provide aid

A Hand may come from the Spiritual Path, the Scholarly Path, or the Keepers. The Hands are therefore a shared service of the Order rather than a separate branch.
